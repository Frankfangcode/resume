從「最快到達」到「更涼地抵達」A cooler journey, alongside a faster one

炎熱天氣下，步行路線也要考慮沿途熱環境。CoolPath 將路線與地表溫度資訊放在一起，讓使用者比較時間、距離與溫度，再選擇適合自己的走法。In hot weather, walking decisions depend on the heat along the way. CoolPath puts routes and land surface temperature together so people can compare time, distance and temperature before choosing.

路線比較與導航Route comparison and navigation

  • 兩條路，一起比較：並列最快與涼爽路線，呈現步行時間、距離及沿線平均地表溫度。Two routes, side by side: walking time, distance and mean land surface temperature.
  • 看見沿途熱環境：在地圖上疊加溫度資訊與路線。See the heat: temperature overlays on the route map.
  • 接續 Google Maps 導航：比較完直接進入導航。Continue in Google Maps: go straight from comparing to navigating.

商業模式｜讓企業 ESG 預算支持城市涼點Business model · ESG funding for cooling stops

  • 企業：以 ESG / CSR 預算認養涼點與獎勵，取得使用人次等影響力資料。Companies: fund cooling stops and rewards through ESG / CSR budgets, with usage data to track impact.
  • 店家：免費加入，提供冷氣、飲水或休息空間，換取人流與消費機會。Shops: join free, offer cooling, water or rest in exchange for foot traffic.
  • 民眾：找涼爽路線與涼點，累積點數兌換獎勵。People: find cooler routes and stops, earn points, redeem rewards.

後續也規劃延伸政府合作，支持城市熱傷害防治。Future plans include government partnerships for urban heat-injury prevention.
